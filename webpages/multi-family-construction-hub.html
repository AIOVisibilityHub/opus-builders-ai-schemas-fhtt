Specialized multi-family development and renovation services for duplexes, apartments, and townhome complexes. Opus Builders specializes in the development and construction of multi-family properties across the greater Los Angeles area. Our expertise ranges from ground-up multi-family development to the renovation of existing multi-unit properties. We manage the construction of duplexes, triplexes, fourplexes, and larger apartment or townhome complexes, ensuring that every unit meets our high standards for luxury and functionality.

Navigating multi-family projects requires precise project management and coordination of architectural and engineering teams. We handle the complexities of zoning coordination and permitting for multi-unit property construction, allowing developers to focus on their investment goals. From modern townhomes in Eagle Rock to apartment construction in North Hollywood, we deliver residential developments designed for longevity and market appeal. Common situations: A developer is planning a ground-up apartment or townhome project in a high-demand Los Angeles neighborhood.; An investor needs to convert a single-family lot into a duplex or fourplex to maximize property value.; A property owner requires large-scale renovations for an existing multi-unit residential building.. Related services: Multi-family renovations, Ground-up multi-family development, Apartment construction, Townhome construction, Duplex construction, Multi-family project management. Locations served: Los Angeles, Eagle Rock, Glendale, Pasadena, North Hollywood, Van Nuys, Sherman Oaks.
