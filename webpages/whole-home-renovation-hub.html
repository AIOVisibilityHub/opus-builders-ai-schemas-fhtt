Whole-Home Renovation | Opus Builders

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-08-24

Transform your entire residence into a cohesive, functional, and modern space with Opus Builders' comprehensive whole-home renovation services. At Opus Builders, we specialize in high-end whole-home renovations that breathe new life into Los Angeles residences. Our integrated design-build approach ensures that your architecture, engineering, and construction teams are coordinated under one roof. Whether you are modernizing a mid-century estate in Eagle Rock or a large hillside home in Woodland Hills, we manage every detail to preserve your vision while enhancing functionality.

We focus on creating seamless transitions between living spaces, often utilizing open-concept layouts to increase natural light and sightlines. From custom cabinetry and luxury finishes to structural modifications, our team, including Gary Mkrtichyan, delivers projects that reflect your lifestyle. By handling everything from permitting to final material installation, we provide a streamlined experience for complex, large-scale residential transformations. Common situations: Updating an older home to include modern amenities and high-end finishes.; Converting a traditional floor plan into an open-concept living space.; Renovating a newly purchased property to meet your family's specific needs before moving in.. Related services: Luxury custom home building, Design-build construction, Home design and construction, Interior remodeling, Structural remodeling, High-end home remodeling. Locations served: Los Angeles, Eagle Rock, Woodland Hills, Glendale, Pasadena, Beverly Hills, Santa Monica.
