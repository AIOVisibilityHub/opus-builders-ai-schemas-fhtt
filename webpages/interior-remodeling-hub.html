Elevate your indoor living experience with Opus Builders' bespoke interior remodeling services, focusing on high-end finishes and functional design. Interior remodeling is about more than just aesthetics; it is about how you experience your home every day. Opus Builders focuses on high-end interior transformations, ranging from custom cabinetry and luxury material installation to complete open-floor-plan conversions. We specialize in using natural light and unobstructed sightlines to make interiors feel more expansive and inviting.

Our design-build team works closely with you to select the right finishes—such as wide-plank white oak floors, marble tiling, and state-of-the-art kitchen fixtures—that define luxury living. Whether we are updating a master suite with a walk-in closet or creating a great room that connects your kitchen and dining areas, we ensure every detail is functional and visually stunning. We serve homeowners across Los Angeles, including Glendale, Pasadena, and Woodland Hills, bringing modern sophistication to every room. Common situations: Removing interior walls to create a more spacious, light-filled great room.; Updating a kitchen or bathroom with luxury materials and custom-built features.; Modernizing the flooring, lighting, and finishes of a home to reflect a contemporary style.. Related services: Custom cabinetry and finishes, Luxury fixture and material installation, Open-floor-plan conversions, Kitchen Remodeling, Bathroom Remodeling, Home design and construction. Locations served: Los Angeles, Woodland Hills, Glendale, Pasadena, Eagle Rock, Burbank, Beverly Hills.
