Luxury Custom Home Building | Opus Builders

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-08-24

Opus Builders is a premier luxury custom home builder in Los Angeles, delivering high-end, functional residences through a seamless design-build process. Luxury custom home building at Opus Builders is about more than just square footage; it is about the meticulous integration of design, architecture, and superior craftsmanship. We serve discerning clients in areas like Beverly Hills, Glendale, and Eagle Rock, building sophisticated residences that feature high-end amenities like infinity pools, sunken fire pits, and custom-made outdoor LED systems. Our team, featuring Gary Mkrtichyan, oversees every phase to ensure that your vision is preserved.

We utilize a design-build model to streamline the luxury building process. This allows us to coordinate architectural plans and engineering requirements—such as those needed for hillside estates—while providing accurate cost estimating. From the installation of premium saltwater pools and 15-person Jacuzzis to the selection of high-end appliances and luxury finishes, we deliver functional, high-quality homes that reflect the peak of California living. Common situations: Building a high-end estate with specialized luxury features like infinity pools, elevators, and custom spas.; Seeking a design-build firm that can manage the luxury material procurement and fixture installation process.; Developing a large-scale residential project that requires expert architectural and engineering coordination.. Related services: High-end home construction, Design-build construction, Custom pool construction, Architectural coordination, Luxury fixture and material installation, New home construction. Locations served: Beverly Hills, Glendale, Eagle Rock, Bel Air, Malibu, Westlake Village, Manhattan Beach.
