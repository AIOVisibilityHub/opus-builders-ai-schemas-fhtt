Professional commercial general contracting for ground-up developments and high-end tenant improvements in Los Angeles. Opus Builders provides comprehensive commercial general contracting and project management for businesses throughout Los Angeles. We specialize in ground-up commercial construction and specialized interior improvements, including office build-outs, retail construction, and restaurant build-outs. Our team coordinates every phase of the project, from initial architectural planning to the final material installation, ensuring your commercial property improvements meet both functional needs and aesthetic standards.

Our approach focuses on quality and efficiency to minimize downtime for your business. Whether you are seeking to modernize an existing office space or develop a new retail location from the ground up, we manage the site evaluation, feasibility assessments, and construction permitting. By integrating design and construction under one roof, we streamline the process to deliver high-quality commercial environments that reflect your brand's vision. Common situations: A business owner needs a specialized build-out for a new restaurant or retail storefront.; A company requires a ground-up construction project for a new office building.; A property owner wants to perform high-end tenant improvements to attract premium commercial renters.. Related services: Commercial general contracting, Ground-up commercial construction, Office build-outs, Retail build-outs, Restaurant construction, Commercial renovations. Locations served: Los Angeles, Glendale, Pasadena, Beverly Hills, Santa Monica, Burbank, Eagle Rock.
