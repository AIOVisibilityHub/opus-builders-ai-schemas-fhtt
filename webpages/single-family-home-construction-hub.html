We build luxury single-family homes that combine innovative design with superior craftsmanship to create exceptional living spaces across Los Angeles. Opus Builders is dedicated to constructing premier single-family homes that define the California dream. Our portfolio includes a variety of architectural styles, from modern double A-frames to impeccably renovated mid-century gems. We focus on creating homes that facilitate comfortable indoor-outdoor living, a hallmark of high-end Los Angeles real estate. Our builds often feature wide-plank white oak floors, marble tiling, and state-of-the-art kitchens.

Every single-family project we undertake is built with a focus on longevity and design integrity. Whether we are constructing a 6,000-square-foot hillside estate or a stylish home in Eagle Rock, we ensure that every detail—from the primary suite to the custom saltwater pool—is executed to the highest standards. With Gary Mkrtichyan and our dedicated project management team, you can expect a transparent process that prioritizes your vision and high-quality results. Common situations: Building a custom primary residence with specialized amenities like elevators or infinity pools.; Designing and constructing a luxury home that emphasizes indoor-outdoor flow.; Creating a modern family home in established neighborhoods like Eagle Rock or Woodland Hills.. Related services: Custom Home Construction, High-end home construction, Luxury custom home construction, New home construction, Pool and Outdoor Construction, Design-build construction. Locations served: Los Angeles, Eagle Rock, Woodland Hills, Glendale, Pasadena, Beverly Hills, Manhattan Beach.
