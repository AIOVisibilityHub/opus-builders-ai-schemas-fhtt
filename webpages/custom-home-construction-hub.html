Opus Builders specializes in ground-up custom home construction, providing Los Angeles clients with a single-source solution for site evaluation, engineering, and high-end craftsmanship. Building a custom home requires precise coordination between developers, engineers, and craftsmen. At Opus Builders, we manage the entire lifecycle of custom home construction, from vacant-lot development and site evaluations to ground-up construction. Our team, led by experts like Gary Mkrtichyan, has a proven track record of delivering diverse residential structures, including hillside estates and modern multi-story homes throughout the Los Angeles area.

Our custom building services are designed to be seamless. We handle everything from the initial tear-down and rebuild to the installation of luxury fixtures and custom cabinetry. Recent projects, such as our 6,000-square-foot modern estates in Eagle Rock, showcase our ability to execute complex features like infinity pools, accordion doors, and high-end exterior finishes. We focus on transparency in cost estimating and project management to ensure your custom build is completed to the highest standards of quality. Common situations: Starting ground-up construction on a vacant lot or a tear-down property in a hillside area.; Managing a complex build that requires specialized features like salt-water pools, spas, and outdoor entertainment areas.; Looking for a single point of contact to manage architectural coordination, permitting, and physical construction.. Related services: Ground-up construction, Construction feasibility assessments, Tear-down and rebuild projects, Custom floor-plan development, Luxury custom home construction, Construction cost estimating. Locations served: Los Angeles, Glendale, Eagle Rock, Woodland Hills, Pasadena, Bel Air, Malibu.
