Construction Cost Estimating | Opus Builders

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-08-24

Type: CollectionPage

Opus Builders provides detailed construction cost estimating to ensure your luxury residential or commercial project remains financially viable and transparent from design to completion. Accurate financial planning is the foundation of any successful luxury build. At Opus Builders, our construction cost estimating service provides transparency and precision, helping you understand the investment required for high-end materials, custom finishes, and complex engineering. We draw on our extensive experience with ground-up construction and whole-home renovations in neighborhoods like Beverly Hills and Woodland Hills to provide realistic figures that account for current market conditions and site-specific needs.

As a design-build firm, we integrate cost estimating into the preconstruction phase. This allows us to align your architectural aspirations with your budget from the start. We detail everything from custom cabinetry and luxury fixtures to site preparation and permitting fees. By providing a clear breakdown of costs, Gary Mkrtichyan and the Opus Builders team ensure you can make informed decisions about your project’s scope and finishes without compromising on quality or design. Common situations: Budgeting for a whole-house gut renovation including marble tile and high-end appliances.; Comparing costs between different luxury finishes and architectural layouts during the design phase.; Estimating the investment for specialized features like salt-water infinity pools or residential elevators.; Determining the financial scope for a multi-family townhome or apartment development.. Related services: Design-Build and Preconstruction, Luxury custom home construction, High-end home remodeling, Construction feasibility assessments, Commercial project management, Multi-family project management. Locations served: Los Angeles, Woodland Hills, Eagle Rock, Beverly Hills, Glendale, Pasadena, Malibu.
