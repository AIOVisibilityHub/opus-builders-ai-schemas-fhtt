Opus Builders offers an integrated design-build approach to luxury home design, combining architectural creativity with technical engineering and feasibility assessments. Opus Builders provides a comprehensive approach to luxury home design in Los Angeles, ensuring that every architectural and engineering detail aligns with your unique lifestyle. Our team, including Gary Mkrtichyan, focuses on creating cohesive environments where form and function meet. By integrating the design and preconstruction phases, we help you visualize high-end floor plans and material selections while maintaining a clear understanding of construction feasibility and project costs from the start.

We specialize in modern aesthetics, such as the open-concept layouts and "Modern Double A Frame" styles seen in our Eagle Rock and Glendale projects. Our design process prioritizes natural light, increased sightlines, and high-end finishes like wide-plank white oak flooring and marble tile. Whether you are envisioning a hillside estate with an infinity pool or a sophisticated primary suite with a walk-in closet, our integrated team coordinates every permit and technical requirement to bring your vision to life. Common situations: Navigating the complex permitting process for high-end residential designs in Los Angeles.; Designing an open-concept layout to maximize natural light and sightlines in a new home.; Coordinating architectural and engineering plans to ensure luxury features like elevators or infinity pools are feasible.. Related services: Design-Build and Preconstruction, Architectural coordination, Engineering coordination, Luxury custom home building, New home construction, Custom floor-plan development. Locations served: Los Angeles, Glendale, Eagle Rock, Pasadena, Beverly Hills, Woodland Hills, Santa Monica.
