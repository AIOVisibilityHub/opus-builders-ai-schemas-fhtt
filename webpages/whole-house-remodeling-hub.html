A whole-house remodel is an opportunity to completely reimagine your living environment. Opus Builders specializes in comprehensive renovations that combine architectural coordination, engineering, and high-end construction for Los Angeles homeowners. When a project requires more than a simple room update, Opus Builders provides expert whole-house remodeling services. We handle complete interior and exterior transformations, ranging from hillside estates in Los Angeles to modern A-frame homes in Eagle Rock. Our team manages every detail, including structural remodeling, plumbing relocation, and custom finishes to ensure the entire residence feels cohesive and intentionally designed.

A whole-home renovation often centers on creating an "enormous great room" or a "Tulum-inspired" aesthetic that connects the kitchen, dining, and living areas. By approaching the house as a single project, we can ensure that high-end amenities—such as walk-in closets in the master suite, custom cabinetry, and seamless indoor-outdoor transitions—are integrated throughout the entire property. Common situations: Executing a gut renovation of a hillside estate to include modern amenities like elevators and accordion doors.; Transforming a dated multi-bedroom house into a modern open-concept home with high-end wood floors and marble tile.; Restoring and modernizing a mid-century home while preserving its architectural integrity.. Related services: Home Remodeling, Interior remodeling, Structural remodeling, Design-build construction, Luxury custom home construction. Locations served: Los Angeles, Eagle Rock, Glendale Marina, Woodland Hills, Pasadena, San Marino.
