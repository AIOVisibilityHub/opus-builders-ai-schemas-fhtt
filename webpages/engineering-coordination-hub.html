Engineering Coordination | Opus Builders

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-08-24

Type: CollectionPage

Opus Builders ensures structural integrity and technical precision through professional engineering coordination for residential and commercial projects. Engineering coordination is vital for the safety and longevity of any high-end construction project, especially in the varied terrain of the Los Angeles area. Opus Builders manages the technical collaboration between structural, civil, and MEP (mechanical, electrical, and plumbing) engineers to ensure all systems are seamlessly integrated into the home's design. This is particularly critical for hillside developments, where advanced engineering is required for stability and drainage.

Our integrated approach means that engineering constraints are addressed early during the design-build phase, preventing costly mid-project changes. Whether we are coordinating the structural requirements for an open-floor-plan conversion or the technical specs for a custom saltwater pool and 15-person jacuzzi, Gary Mkrtichyan and our project management team ensure that every technical detail supports the overall aesthetic and functional goals of the property. Common situations: Coordinating structural engineering for expansive open-concept living areas and vaulted ceilings.; Managing civil engineering requirements for complex hillside estates and infinity pool installations.; Integrating modern mechanical and plumbing systems into older home modernizations or gut renovations.. Related services: Construction feasibility assessments, Structural remodeling, Luxury custom home building, Pool and Outdoor Construction, Custom spa construction, Multi-family project management. Locations served: Los Angeles, Glendale, Eagle Rock, Pasadena, Woodland Hills, Calabasas.
