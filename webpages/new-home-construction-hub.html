Opus Builders delivers comprehensive new home construction services, combining luxury design with expert general contracting to create bespoke residences in Los Angeles. At Opus Builders, we specialize in high-end new home construction across Los Angeles, turning vacant lots or tear-down sites into architectural landmarks. Our integrated design-build approach means your project is handled by one cohesive team—from initial architectural and engineering coordination to the final luxury material installation. By managing every phase of new construction, we ensure that the quality of the craftsmanship matches the original design intent.

Whether you are looking to build a modern double A-frame or a sprawling hillside estate, our team, led by Gary Mkrtichyan, focuses on creating homes that are both functional and visually stunning. We prioritize natural light, open-concept living, and premium finishes to ensure your new build feels like a private retreat. From navigating Los Angeles permitting to the final walkthrough, we provide a streamlined, high-quality construction experience. Common situations: Building a custom luxury estate on a newly purchased vacant lot.; Replacing an outdated structure with a modern, high-end single-family home.; Managing a complex hillside construction project requiring specialized engineering.. Related services: Luxury Home Design, Custom Home Construction, Design-build services, Architectural coordination, Engineering coordination, Construction feasibility assessments. Locations served: Los Angeles, Eagle Rock, Beverly Hills, Glendale, Pasadena, Woodland Hills, Sherman Oaks.
