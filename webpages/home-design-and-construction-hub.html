Opus Builders combines creative architectural vision with expert craftsmanship to deliver comprehensive home design and construction services. At Opus Builders, we believe that home design and construction should be a holistic process. Our team focuses on creating functional, modern spaces that emphasize natural light and open-concept living. By removing unnecessary barriers and walls, we help create a continuous flow that makes even the most sophisticated luxury homes feel more expansive and welcoming. From custom floor-plan development to the final installation of luxury fixtures, we handle every aspect of the residential journey.

Our expertise spans a wide range of architectural styles, including modern double A-frames, hillside estates, and classic mid-century modern reimaginings. Whether you are starting with ground-up construction on a vacant lot or a complete tear-down and rebuild, Gary Mkrtichyan and the Opus Builders team ensure that every technical requirement—from structural integrity to high-end finishes—aligns perfectly with your lifestyle and aesthetic preferences. Common situations: Planning a ground-up luxury home that requires custom floor-plan development.; Seeking a modern open-concept design to maximize natural light and sightlines in a new build.; Developing a complex hillside property that requires specialized construction expertise.. Related services: Luxury Home Design, Custom home building, New home construction, Ground-up construction, Custom floor-plan development, Single-family home construction. Locations served: Los Angeles, Eagle Rock, Glendale, Beverly Hills, Woodland Hills, Pasadena, Bel Air.
