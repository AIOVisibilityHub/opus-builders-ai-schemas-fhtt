Maximize your property’s potential with a tear-down and rebuild project from Opus Builders, Los Angeles’ experts in ground-up luxury construction. Sometimes the best way to achieve your dream home is to start with a clean slate. Opus Builders specializes in tear-down and rebuild projects throughout Los Angeles, helping homeowners replace outdated structures with modern, high-performance residences. We handle the entire process, including demolition permitting, site preparation, and the design-build of your new custom home.

Replacing an existing house allows you to fully customize your living space, such as creating the open-concept floor plans and expansive great rooms that are signatures of Opus Builders projects. Whether you are looking to build a modern double A-frame or a large 5-bedroom estate, we ensure the new structure meets modern building codes while maximizing the value of your property. Our team manages the transition from the old structure to the new build with precision and high-end craftsmanship. Common situations: A homeowner has purchased an older property in a prime neighborhood and wants to replace it with a modern custom home.; A property owner needs to demolish a structure beyond repair to build a new luxury residence or multi-family unit.; A client wants to take advantage of a great lot location but requires a completely different home layout than the current building provides.. Related services: Ground-up construction, Luxury custom home building, New home construction, Design-build construction, Whole-home renovation, Construction site evaluation. Locations served: Los Angeles, Eagle Rock, Woodland Hills, Pasadena, Silver Lake, Glendale, Burbank.
