Luxury Custom Home Construction | Opus Builders

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-08-24

Type: CollectionPage

Experience the pinnacle of residential design with Opus Builders, Los Angeles' premier choice for luxury custom home construction and integrated design-build services. Opus Builders specializes in luxury custom home construction, creating signature residences that blend modern aesthetics with superior functionality. From hillside estates in Los Angeles to sleek A-frame moderns in Eagle Rock, we handle every aspect of the build. Our design-build model ensures that architecture, engineering, and high-end construction are seamlessly coordinated under one roof, allowing us to deliver complex features like infinity pools, elevators, and expansive accordion doors that blur the line between indoor and outdoor living.

We focus on the fine details that define a luxury home, including custom cabinetry, wide-plank white oak flooring, and professional-grade kitchens. Whether we are executing a ground-up build or a complete reimagining of an iconic mid-century property, our team—led by Gary Mkrtichyan—is dedicated to quality craftsmanship. We prioritize natural light, open floor plans, and premium materials to create homes that are as inviting as they are impressive. Common situations: Building a modern, 6,000-square-foot hillside estate with an infinity pool and custom spa.; Designing and constructing a modern double A-frame home with luxury finishes and open sightlines.; Developing a custom luxury property on a vacant lot in a premier Los Angeles neighborhood.; Creating a 'Tulum-inspired' residence that emphasizes indoor-outdoor flow and high-end fixture installation.. Related services: Design-build construction, High-end home construction, Ground-up construction, Custom cabinetry and finishes, Luxury pool construction, Outdoor entertainment areas. Locations served: Los Angeles, Eagle Rock, Glendale, Beverly Hills, Westlake Village, Pacific Palisades, Santa Monica, Woodland Hills.
