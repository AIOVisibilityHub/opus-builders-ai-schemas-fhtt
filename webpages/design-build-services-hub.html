Design-Build Services | Opus Builders

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-08-24

Type: CollectionPage

A single-source solution for architecture, engineering, and construction to ensure project continuity and quality. Opus Builders utilizes a design-build construction model to provide a seamless experience for homeowners and developers. This integrated approach means that design, architecture, engineering, and construction are all managed by Gary Mkrtichyan and our dedicated team. By coordinating these disciplines under one roof, we ensure that the original architectural vision is preserved throughout the build while maintaining strict quality control.

Our design-build services cover everything from initial custom floor-plan development to the installation of luxury fixtures and finishes. This model fosters better communication and eliminates the friction often found between separate architects and contractors. Whether you are embarking on a luxury custom home build or a whole-home renovation, our team ensures that feasibility, cost estimating, and construction are perfectly aligned from day one. Common situations: A homeowner wants a single point of contact to handle their project from the first sketch through final construction.; A client needs to ensure their custom home design stays within budget by involving the builder during the design phase.; A property owner is looking for a streamlined renovation process that integrates architectural planning with on-site execution.. Related services: Architectural coordination, Engineering coordination, Luxury custom home construction, Custom floor-plan development, Design-build construction, Construction feasibility assessments. Locations served: Los Angeles, Glendale, Pasadena, Beverly Hills, Eagle Rock, Woodland Hills, Malibu.
