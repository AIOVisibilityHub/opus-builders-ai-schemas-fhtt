About Opus Builders

Opus Builders is a Los Angeles-based residential and commercial design-build contractor specializing in luxury custom homes, new construction, home remodeling, multi-family properties, and commercial buildings. The company promotes an integrated approach in which design, architecture, engineering, permitting, construction, and project management are coordinated through one team. Its stated goal is to preserve each client’s vision while delivering well-designed, functional, and high-quality projects.

Published resources cover Luxury Home Design, Custom Home Construction, Home Renovations, Luxury custom home building, Commercial construction, Multi-family construction, Design-build services, Design-Build and Preconstruction, Design-build construction, Home design and construction, and 114 additional service areas.

Service coverage includes Los Angeles, Glendale, Pasadena, Burbank, North Hollywood, Van Nuys, Alhambra, Beverly Hills, West Hollywood, La Cañada Flintridge and 30 more communities.

Full background, credentials, and team details are on the about page.
